Design Thinking Project Showcase

1. EMPATHIZE

College engineering students face cognitive overload: 6+ concurrent subjects, weekly lab records, surprise quizzes, semester exams, and placement prep. Students struggle with procrastination and panic-cramming.

2. DEFINE

Existing tools (Google Calendar, To-Do lists) are static and rigid. When a student misses one scheduled study block, the entire timetable collapses with no dynamic recovery or concept assistance.

3. IDEATE

Combine AI algorithmic prioritization (urgency × difficulty matrix) + automatic schedule division + adaptive missed-task rebalancing + an in-app AI concept explainer (Athena).

4. PROTOTYPE (This Web/Mobile App)

Built with 7 functional modules: Live Dashboard, AI Planner, Task Matrix, Athena Concept Explainer, Gamified Streak Tracker, Smart Reminders, and 1-Click Adaptive Rebalance.

5. TEST & DEMONSTRATE

Demonstrate on stage: Click "Simulate Missed Task" to see real-time AI schedule adaptation, ask Athena to explain "Virtual Memory" in ELI5 mode, and toggle the mobile frame!
